GST Return Filing: GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B Explained

What is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is the return of your outward supplies, meaning the sales invoices you have issued. The details you report here are used to build your customers' input tax credit.

What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is a summary return in which you declare your total sales, claim input tax credit and pay the GST liability for the period.

Monthly or quarterly?

Eligible small taxpayers can opt for the quarterly return scheme (QRMP), paying tax monthly and filing returns quarterly. Other taxpayers file every month. Your turnover and registration type decide which option applies to you.

Good habits

  • Reconcile purchases with GSTR-2B before claiming input credit
  • Issue correct tax invoices on time
  • File before the due date to avoid late fees and interest
  • Keep invoices and records organised for at least the required period

Due dates

Due dates differ by return type and filing frequency, and they are sometimes extended. Check the GST portal for the current schedule.
