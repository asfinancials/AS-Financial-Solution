ITR Filing: Who Should File an Income Tax Return?

Who should file an ITR?

You generally need to file if your total income is above the basic exemption limit for your age and tax regime. Filing can also be required in other cases, such as holding foreign assets, large bank deposits or high-value transactions, or when you want to claim a refund or carry forward losses. Even when filing is not mandatory, a return is useful proof of income for loans and visas.

Documents to keep ready

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 from your employer (if salaried)
  • Form 26AS and AIS/TIS from the income-tax portal
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • Investment proofs for deductions (80C, 80D, etc.)
  • Capital gains statements, rent receipts and business records, where applicable

Common mistakes

  • Not reporting interest income or other-source income
  • Mismatch between Form 26AS/AIS and the return
  • Choosing the wrong ITR form
  • Forgetting to e-verify the return after filing

Deadlines

The due date depends on the type of taxpayer and can change, so always check the official income-tax portal for the current date. Filing late can attract fees and interest.
